Euler diagnostics
equation / numerical flux

DG/FVM lab

Euler cylinder solves the two-dimensional compressible Euler equations with a cell-centred finite-volume scheme, equivalently DG P0. The obstacle is an embedded reflective wall on a Cartesian mesh.

U=(ρ,ρu,ρv,E), F(U) and G(U) are Euler fluxes, and p=(γ−1)(E−ρ(u²+v²)/2). The face flux is local Lax–Friedrichs/Rusanov: F̂=½(F_L+F_R)−½α a_max(U_R−U_L).

Nx, Ny are the mesh dimensions. CFL controls the explicit time step. spf is solver steps per animation frame. M∞ is freestream Mach number. R is cylinder radius. γ is the ratio of specific heats.

The scalar cases remain DG demonstrations: Q0 is the finite-volume limit; Q1–Q3 add Legendre modes inside each cell; jumps are measured at element faces; Burgers can use a mean-preserving troubled-cell minmod limiter.

Hotkeys: Space run/pause, R reset, S one step, [/] degree, 1 Euler cylinder, 2 scalar advection, 3 Burgers.
