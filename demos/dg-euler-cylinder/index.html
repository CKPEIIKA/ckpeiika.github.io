Euler diagnostics

    DG lab

    Euler cylinder solves the two-dimensional compressible Euler equations with a modal DG scheme. The obstacle is an embedded reflective wall on a Cartesian mesh.

    U=(ρ,ρu,ρv,E), F(U) and G(U) are Euler fluxes, and p=(γ−1)(E−ρ(u²+v²)/2). The face flux is local Lax–Friedrichs/Rusanov: F̂=½(F_L+F_R)−½α a_max(U_R−U_L).

    Nx, Ny are the mesh dimensions. CFL controls the explicit time step. spf is solver steps per animation frame. M∞ is freestream Mach number. R is cylinder radius. γ is the ratio of specific heats.

    All cases use DG modal coefficients. P0/Q0 is the finite-volume limit; higher degrees add Legendre modes inside each cell. Burgers can use a mean-preserving troubled-cell minmod limiter, while Euler uses a positivity fallback for density and pressure.

    Hotkeys: Space run/pause, R reset, S one step, [/] degree, 1 Euler, 2 diamond, 3 advection, 4 Burgers.
