VERIFIED NUMERICAL MODEL

DG / FV laboratory

One modal engine, four equation families: compressible Euler flow over an embedded body, periodic diamond translation, scalar advection, and inviscid Burgers. P0 is the finite-volume limit of the same weak form.

Deterministic and verified against invariants and limiting states. Not validated against an experimental configuration. The Euler body is stair-stepped on a Cartesian mesh.
